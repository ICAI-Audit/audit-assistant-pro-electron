## Observed workflow

1. Set client, financial/assessment year, business/profession facts, applicability and 3CA/3CB selection.

2. Complete acceptance and manually maintained compliance tracker.

3. Review prefilled clause values and sources, enter structured fields/tables and retain overrides.

4. Link supporting evidence and document internal review notes or report-facing qualifications.

5. Review programme, responsibility acknowledgement and readiness exceptions.

6. Generate internal Markdown report pack and draft 3CA/3CB sections and 3CD Word/Excel/PDF/HTML paths as supported.

## Form No. 26 (T-067, 23 November 2026)

This section is newer than the snapshot above, which describes Forms 3CA, 3CB and 3CD as reviewed on 30 October 2026 and still holds for them. Feature F-032. Decisions D-014 and D-015. State on 23 November 2026: built and automated verified on synthetic data (unit suite 230 files / 4,746 tests; Tax Audit application test 301 of 301 twice, `--short` 26 of 26 twice), uncommitted on `feat/tax-audit-form-26`, not owner-accepted, no UAT. The independent verification (task 29) passed after two fixes (FIX-T29): every fixed row of a row list shows its labels, and every file goes through the system Save As window.

### Two forms, chosen by year

The module now holds two forms. The choice is made at the top of the Tax Audit page, before any Form 3CD code runs ([src/lib/taxAuditForm26Year.ts](../../../src/lib/taxAuditForm26Year.ts), [TaxAuditFormChooser.tsx](../../../src/components/tax-audit/form26/TaxAuditFormChooser.tsx)). The page's own code is otherwise unchanged; `src/hooks/useTaxAudit.ts` is not edited.

- Tax year beginning on or after 1 April 2026 (an engagement year such as 2026-27 or `FY 2027-28`): Form No. 26, under the Income-tax Act, 2025 and rule 47 of the Income-tax Rules, 2026. Sources: [sources and rules](../SOURCES_AND_RULES.md#tax-audit-form-no-26-t-067).
- An earlier tax year: Forms 3CA, 3CB and 3CD, exactly as before.
- A year the application cannot read: the message "The tax audit form cannot be chosen", with a way to the engagements; nothing is created. If a Form 3CD audit already exists for that engagement, Form 3CD opens as before.
- An engagement of 2026-27 or later that already holds a Form 3CD audit is converted (below); the earlier draft is kept and can be viewed read-only.
- The release switch `FORM26_RELEASED` is `true`. Set to `false`, every engagement would get Form 3CD as before.

### What the form holds

Form No. 26 follows the office's fillable Form 26 as it stands (D-014). It is generated from an exact extraction of that file into [src/data/taxAuditForm26Definition.ts](../../../src/data/taxAuditForm26Definition.ts): 57 clause units (Category, Parts A and B, particulars 1 to 8, clauses 9 to 53, Signature), 142 answers, 65 tables and 55 schedules; the same preset answers (63 "No", 4 "Yes" and 9 others), the same calculated columns and totals, and the same rules for which schedule opens on which answer. Five slips of the file's mechanics are not copied (D-015): every table that takes rows can delete a row; schedule 39's second calculated column uses the fresh value of the first; counts refresh after a row is added or deleted; schedule 53(b)(ii) can be reached; "Show all schedules" changes what is shown and counted, not what is exported. The catalogue: [tax audit field catalogue](../TAX_AUDIT_FIELD_CATALOG.md#form-no-26-t-067).

Storage uses the existing tax-audit tables; no table or column was added. One audit row per engagement, with the statutory version `ITA_2025_FORM_26_RULE_47_NOTIF_22_2026`; one row per clause unit (`f26_<unit>`, 57) and one per table (`f26_t_<table>`, 65): 122 rows. Working-paper references read `TA-F26-<number>`. The category of assessee is held in the audit's setup; `form_type` is derived from it ('3CA' for category (i), '3CB' for (ii)). These keys are for ever (a test of frozen ids guards them).

### The workspace

The present Tax Audit layout is kept. The header shows the client and year, the badges Form No. 26, Income-tax Act, 2025, Rule 47 and the category, the save line and "Next unfilled" with its count, and one sentence on why this form applies. Three views sit beside the title: Clauses, What changed and Review Summary.

- Clause list: search, the chips All, Unfilled, Evidence, Qualified and Reviewed, the groups of the form, and a "View" button with three switches (highlight unfilled; show all schedules; show Form 3CD notes). Labels of clauses 9 to 53 are the Subject entries of the office's mapping; the editor always shows the form's own wording.
- Clause editor: the form's wording and its answers; schedules and tables open under their clause; long tables become a row list with one row open; "Prepared", "Reviewed" and "Approve", and "Edit" on an approved clause; review remarks; the qualification box, with "Add to the report's observations" for clauses 9 to 53.
- Right pane, "Sources, Evidence and Review": the page's evidence rail; "Last year" (the same client's engagement of the year before, read only, in whichever form it used); "3CD draft" for a converted audit.
- Name, PAN and tax year are filled from the client master, marked "From client master" until edited. Nothing else is prefilled.
- Saving: when a field is left, and after a short wait for a table; one save at a time per row. A change by another user to the same answer shows "Changed by someone else at HH:MM. Keep mine · Use theirs". A clause approved while someone types shows "This clause was approved while you were typing. Your entry was not saved." Closing the window with an unsaved change holds the close, saves, and says "All changes are saved. You can close the window now."
- Approve and unlock are open to every user assigned to the engagement, as on Form 3CD (D-015, A6; GAP-25).

### Comparison with Form 3CD

Under each question a quiet line reads "Was Form 3CD clause N · what changed", or "New in Form No. 26.", from the mapping ([src/data/taxAuditForm26Mapping.ts](../../../src/data/taxAuditForm26Mapping.ts)): 52 rows (6 new, 33 changed, 13 the same) and 15 Form 3CD clauses with no place in Form 26. The rows shown are those corrected from the official texts; three cells that could not be confirmed show the office's entry (D-014). "Show Form 3CD notes" is on by default for 2026-27 only and is remembered per user and engagement on this computer. "What changed" lists the rows with filters and downloads a briefing in Word. No comparison text enters an exported form.

### Outputs of Form No. 26

From Review Summary: the Word file, the PDF, the working-paper pack (Word: every clause with its answers, tables, evidence, review state, remarks and qualification) and the save file (`.json`, the office file's own format). Every file is saved through the window's own "Save As" (FIX-T29): the user chooses where first, and the screen says the file was saved only once it is written; a cancelled "Save As" says "Not saved.". "Load a save file" reads a file saved by the office's fillable form and shows what it will change (counts, a different PAN or name, blanks, approved clauses that are skipped). "Replace the answers" then asks where to save a copy of the audit as it is (`<file>_before-import.json`) and writes nothing until that copy is saved; if the user cancels, or the copy cannot be written, nothing is replaced and the dialog says "Nothing was changed: the copy of the present answers was not saved." (or "… could not be saved."). A file the audit already matches replaces nothing and asks for no copy. The "What changed" briefing and the read-only view's save file go through "Save As" the same way. No Form No. 26 output carries a draft notice (the owner's ruling of 22 November 2026); the Form 3CD exports keep theirs. The export follows the office's own Word export block for block; the PDF draws the tick boxes and prints ₹ as "Rs." (GAP-44).

### Conversion of a Form 3CD draft

When an engagement of 2026-27 or later already holds a Form 3CD audit, opening Tax Audit creates the Form No. 26 audit at once.

- An untouched Form 3CD draft (only its automatic prefill) converts with no questions and no notice.
- Otherwise seven answers of the same substance in the corrected mapping come across: name and PAN, clauses 10, 15(a), 15(b), 19 and 22. Each is marked "Brought from the 3CD draft", has "Confirm", and counts as unfilled until confirmed. Other clauses 9 to 53 whose Form 3CD clause has an answer say "The earlier Form 3CD draft has an answer for this clause. See it", which opens the "3CD draft" tab. Clauses that receive nothing start with the form's presets.
- A short notice above the panes says what happened and links to "View the earlier 3CD draft" (49 clauses, read only). It can be dismissed.
- A half-finished conversion is completed once, on the next opening; an answer typed since is never overwritten.
- No Form 3CD row, clause row, evidence link or acceptance check is ever changed or deleted. Evidence linked to the draft stays with it (GAP-40).
- If the engagement's year is changed back to before 2026-27, Form 3CD opens with a notice that a Form No. 26 draft exists and a read-only view of it, with its save file.

### Known limits of Form No. 26

- No portal file: no official schema or utility exists for Form No. 26 (GAP-24). The content awaits the domain team's review ([domain review](../TAX_AUDIT_FORM26_DOMAIN_REVIEW.md); GAP-08).
- Not offered for Form No. 26 in this release: the Overview, Setup, Acceptance, Compliance and Programme tabs of Form 3CD, and an Excel export (GAP-41).
- The lock of an approved clause is on screen only; the gaps of concurrent use are GAP-35 and GAP-36.
- "Show all schedules" is shared by every user of the audit, as in the office's file (GAP-38).
- At 1280 × 720 the longest list labels (clauses 12 to 14 and 51 to 53) leave fewer than six whole entries in view; with the conversion notice showing, clauses 14 and 27 have an editor under 300 px and clause 52 three list entries (GAP-46). Dismissing the notice gives the room back.
- The working-paper pack has a section for each of the 57 clauses, so a short audit gives about 27 pages. Word draws "☑" as a filled ticked square beside a plain "☐".
- In a production build, View > Reload with an unsaved change is held like a close: the page stays, and the sentence says the window can be closed.
- The audit row's name and PAN are set once, when the audit is created; they do not follow later edits of particulars 1 and 3.
