## F-032 Tax Audit Form No. 26 (T-067, 21 to 23 November 2026)

Status: **Automated verified** on synthetic data only, on `feat/tax-audit-form-26` from `main` at `05e35db`, uncommitted (23 November 2026). Not owner-accepted. No UAT. The independent verification (build plan task 29) passed after fixes (two defects found and fixed, below). Still to come: the owner's hand check, and the commit on his word. Decisions: D-014 (what to build), D-015 (the build plan and the later rulings).

What it does. Tax Audit now chooses the form by the engagement's year. A tax year that begins on or after 1 April 2026 (2026-27 and later) opens Form No. 26 under the Income-tax Act, 2025 and rule 47 of the Income-tax Rules, 2026. An earlier year opens Forms 3CA, 3CB and 3CD exactly as before. A year the application cannot read gives a plain message and creates nothing; if a Form 3CD audit already exists for such an engagement, Form 3CD opens as before. Form No. 26 follows the office's fillable Form 26 as it stands (D-014): its wording, Parts, clauses, schedules and tables, its preset answers, its totals and calculations, and which schedule opens on which answer. Five mechanical slips of that file are not copied (D-015). The present Tax Audit layout is kept: the clause list with search and filter chips, "Next unfilled", the clause editor with Prepared, Reviewed and Approve, review remarks and the qualification box, and the evidence pane with a "Last year" tab. The header shows Form No. 26, Income-tax Act, 2025, Rule 47 and the category. Three views: Clauses, What changed (the Form 26 / Form 3CD mapping as corrected from the official texts) and Review Summary (what is left to fill, one line per review state, the downloads). Name, PAN and tax year are filled from the client master and marked "From client master". Answers are saved when a field is left (a table after a short wait); a change made by another user at the same time shows "Keep mine · Use theirs"; a clause approved while someone types refuses the entry and says so. Outputs: Word, PDF, the working-paper pack (Word) and the save file (`.json` in the office file's own format); a save file made by the office's fillable form can be loaded. No Form No. 26 output carries a draft notice (the owner's ruling of 22 November 2026); the Form 3CD exports keep theirs. An engagement of 2026-27 or later that already holds a Form 3CD audit is converted: the Form 26 audit is created; an untouched Form 3CD draft converts with no questions; otherwise seven answers of the same substance (name, PAN, clauses 10, 15(a), 15(b), 19 and 22) come across marked "Brought from the 3CD draft" and count as filled only once confirmed, and other clauses show the earlier answer beside them; the Form 3CD rows are never changed and can be viewed read-only. Form 3CD itself (F-019, F-020, F-021) is unchanged.

- Partial: some implementation exists but a specific functional or control gap is visible.
- Prototype: active screen uses sample state or non-persisting controls for its core behaviour.
- Not established: no verified implementation path was identified in this bounded review. This is not an exhaustive proof of absence.
- Automated verified: named checks passed at a stated commit and environment. State the tested boundary.
- UAT accepted: a named reviewer accepted stated scenarios on a stated build. No application feature has this status from this review.

All rows below have runtime verification pending unless stated otherwise. Evidence paths are navigational pointers. For a changed feature, add its detailed contract using the task template, test evidence and owner acceptance.
