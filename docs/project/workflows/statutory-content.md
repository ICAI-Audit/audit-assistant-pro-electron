# Maintain statutory rules and audit wording

## When to use

Use for applicability, tax-audit fields, forms, CARO, standards, thresholds or report wording.

## Read before acting

Read SOURCES_AND_RULES, the affected rule code and tests, statutory version constants, form selection, structured schemas, readiness and exports. Obtain the actual authoritative sources for the target period.

## Procedure

1. Identify jurisdiction, entity, financial year, assessment year, legal effective dates and transitional facts. Do not substitute a prior-year quick reference for current authority.

2. Record rule ID, exact provision/page, official URL or controlled source, version, checked date and reviewer. Distinguish legal requirement, guidance and interpretation.

3. Compare current and revised logic clause by clause within scope. Identify consequences for fields, applicability, readiness, prefill, warnings, report wording and every export.

4. Preserve the basis of historical engagements. Test dates and values below/equal/above boundaries, not-applicable and unknown cases, and unsupported assessment years.

5. Label drafts and professional-review requirements. Verify portal schema separately if portal compatibility is claimed. Obtain recorded professional content approval before marking it accepted.

## Required result

A versioned source-to-rule change record and focused tests. If the source or interpretation remains unresolved, record the uncertainty and do not manufacture legal authority.
