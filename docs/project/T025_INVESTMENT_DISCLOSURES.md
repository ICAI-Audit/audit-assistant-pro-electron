## Result and information flow

1. Existing mapped TB rows establish each investment note and its populated Level 3 sub-classifications. `investmentPeriodRows` retains ledger identity, combines an unambiguous comparative identity, and retains separate PY-only ledgers. Source debit-negative investment balances become positive disclosure amounts; contra signs are preserved. With a separate PY TB, its closing values supply comparatives; PY-off starts with zero comparative values. Manual PY is allocated by the preparer and reconciled to the note total.
2. Notes → current/non-current investments → Fill Details builds a table for every populated sub-classification. Each dropdown contains only that group's ledgers, identified by TB/row IDs and branch/PY-only labels, so duplicate ledger names do not collapse identities.
3. Each detail row records printed investee name, instrument category, optional custom category, nature, Holding/Subsidiary/Associate/Joint Venture/Controlled special purpose entity/None relationship, quotation, valuation basis, CY/PY carrying and market values, and diminution. Securities have CY/PY quantities, face value and paid status; partly paid investments also have paid-per-unit and uncalled liability. Non-current rows require trade/other classification; current rows never print it.
4. Partnership firm rows additionally record firm total capital, company share in capital, reporting date, capital basis and date-difference/adjustment explanation, plus every partner's name, CY/PY capital and profit share. LLP is a separate corporate investment category; it cannot be saved under a partnership sub-classification. Redemption disclosures capture dates, the amount within twelve months and explanation; non-current amounts within twelve months trigger review rather than changing TB mapping.
5. The main-process `investments:save` checks client/year permissions, the TB-to-year link, locked/final state, numeric validity and actual ledger membership in the selected note/sub-classification. Comparative source TB IDs must belong to the same client and immediately preceding year. Current rows lose any stale trade value. Unknown/incomplete fields remain drafts with explicit review warnings; fabricated or stale ledger identities and invalid numbers are rejected.
6. Per-TB details use `note_details`; consolidated details use `entity_note_details`. Both use reserved titles `Investment disclosure FY <entityPeriodId> <kind>` and negative numbers starting below -3000000, distinct from live note numbers and depreciation keys. Generic note saves cannot overwrite this namespace. No schema change, database reset, reseed or rewrite of existing note records is required.
7. Notes, PDF and financial Excel share narrow disclosure tables, securities details, partnership tables, valuation and aggregate summaries. The existing note total stays authoritative. Disclosures never inject balances, subtract diminution again or plug a mismatch. Counts, percentages and per-unit rupee amounts are not denomination-scaled; financial amounts are scaled. Missing details, stale mappings and amount differences are printed as review warnings.
8. The consolidated Fill Details workbook includes separate current/non-current sheets and all investment/partner fields, with row IDs linking partners to investments. Custom categories round-trip. Upload routes through the same year-scoped validated save path. Blank sheets do not overwrite saved records; investment parsing/save failures are surfaced, with an explicit warning that preceding sheets may already have saved.
